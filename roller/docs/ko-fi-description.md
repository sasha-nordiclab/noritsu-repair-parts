Noritsu QSF-V50 Rack Roller: 3D-printable, with a grippy TPU sleeve

A replacement film transport roller for the racks of the Noritsu QSF-V50 C-41 film processor. Worn, cracked or missing roller? Print a new one: a rigid PETG core on the Ø6 mm shaft and a grippy TPU "rubber" sleeve with a raised diamond grid.

WHAT YOU GET
• Print files (STEP), ready for the slicer:
  – PETG core, print-optimised (8 anti-rotation ribs)
  – TPU sleeve for it, as a flat sheet
  – TPU sleeve for the classic core (4 ribs, flat flanges), as a flat sheet
  – Smooth TPU sleeves (no grid, 1.6 mm, flush with the flanges) for both cores, as flat sheets
  – Dovetail version: a core with a dovetail slot and a sleeve whose ends lock together into a dovetail key, slid on from one end
• Reference STEP files: both rollers with the sleeve fitted, the fitted sleeves on their own, and the classic core
• The parametric FreeCAD model: every size is in one spreadsheet, so you can adapt the roller to another rack
• Renders, dimensions and print notes on GitHub

HOW IT WORKS
• Core: Ø26.5 mm running surface, Ø30 mm flanges, 60 mm between the flanges, Ø6.5 mm bore that runs freely on the Ø6 mm shaft. Inside, four spokes and a mid-length partition keep it light and stiff; 5 mm recesses around the hub at both ends.
• Sleeve: a 1.2 mm TPU base with a 1 mm raised diamond grid (ribs 1.2 mm wide at ±45°, 3 mm pitch). Grooves on the inside key onto the ribs of the core, so the sleeve cannot turn on it. The flanges hold it in place along the axis.
• Printed flat: the sleeve is printed as a sheet whose length matches the roller circumference at the neutral layer of the rubber, so it closes without stretching. Its seam lands exactly on a rib, and the grid continues across the seam.

PRINT SETTINGS (sliced in OrcaSlicer, Bambu Lab A1)
Core
• Material: PETG. The C-41 baths run at 38 °C; PLA softens and creeps there.
• Orientation: standing on its bottom end face, as exported. No supports needed: every overhang is 40° or less.
• 0.4 mm nozzle, layer height 0.2 mm, line width 0.6 mm. All walls are multiples of 0.6 mm, so they print as solid perimeters.
• About 1 h 25 min and 25 g.
Sleeve
• Material: TPU (95A or similar).
• Orientation: flat, smooth grooved side on the plate, grid up. No supports needed.
• Made for a 0.6 mm nozzle: the grid ribs are exactly two 0.6 mm lines wide with vertical walls, so each layer is a continuous path with no tips and far fewer travel moves for TPU to string. A 0.4 mm nozzle works too.
• Layer height 0.2 mm, line width 0.6 mm, slow TPU speeds.
• About 1 h 20 min and 11–12 g with a 0.6 mm nozzle.

ASSEMBLY
1. Slide the core onto the shaft.
2. Lay the sheet around the core with the grooves over the ribs.
3. Close it so that the seam lands on a rib. The flanges at both ends keep the edges in place.
4. If the seam lifts, a thin line of flexible CA glue along it holds it closed.
